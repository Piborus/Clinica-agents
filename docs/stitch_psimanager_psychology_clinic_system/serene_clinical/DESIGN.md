## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted to project clinical rigor balanced with profound psychological warmth. Designed specifically for mental health professionals, clinics, and their patients, the visual environment balances therapeutic tranquility, utmost discretion, and professional administrative mastery.

### Emotional Resonance

- **Sanctuary & Safety:** Creates an immediate sense of calm and ethical safety, assuring practitioners and patients of confidential, protected data.
- **Cognitive Ease:** Minimizes emotional and cognitive fatigue during extended administrative tasks, anamnesis recording, and schedule coordination.
- **Human Warmth:** Balances clinical precision with organic undertones to counteract the cold, detached aesthetics typical of legacy medical software.

### Visual Style: Warm Professional Minimalist

The aesthetic blends **warm organic minimalism** with **modern clinical precision**:

- Restrained, generous negative space providing visual respite.
- Subtle tactile surfaces using soft boundary definitions rather than hard dividers.
- Rich, muted organic tones (deep petrol slate, sage leaf, soft mint) establishing an empathetic atmosphere suitable for daily clinical immersion.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intentional equilibrium between psychological reassurance and operational clarity.

### Primary Role: Deep Petrol Slate (`#1E3A4C`)

Serves as the structural anchor. Applied to primary CTAs, active global navigation, key brand signifiers, and high-emphasis interface headers. It communicates security, medical authority, and quiet confidence.

### Secondary Role: Sage Herbal (`#4E7D6B`)

The therapeutic accent. Used for secondary actions, interactive highlights, positive confirmations, and focused states that require distinction without visual aggression.

### Tertiary & Surface Accents: Soft Mint (`#E2EFE9`) & Ice Wash (`#EBF1F5`)

Low-saturation tints utilized for card highlights, active table selections, appointment pill backgrounds, and subtle callout containers.

### Neutrals & Foundation

- **Canvas Base:** Soft off-white (`#F8FAF9`) prevents retinal glare during evening note-taking.
- **Card Surfaces:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for distinct visual prioritization of patient files and calendars.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Soft neutral mist (`#E2E8F0`) providing whisper-thin structural organization.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Graphite primary (`#1E293B`) for clinical notes and titles; slate secondary (`#475569`) for metadata, hints, and timestamps; muted tertiary (`#94A3B8`) for placeholders and inactive captions.

### Contextual & Status Feedback

- **Confirmed / Paid / Active:** Balanced Emerald (`#10B981`)
- **Pending / Attention Required:** Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`)
- **Teleconsultation / Video Link:** Clinical Blue (`#3B82F6`)
- **Urgent / Risk / Cancelled:** Gentle Crimson (`#EF4444`)

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its balanced geometric clarity, open letterforms, and warm humanist terminals.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Clinical Reading

- **Long-Form Anamnesis & Medical Records:** Body copy utilizes a calculated 1.625 line-height ratio (`body-lg` at 26px line height, `body-md` at 22px) to prevent vertical eye-strain during extended reading of psychological histories.
- **Numbers & Scheduling Data:** Use proportional tabular alignment in data grids to keep dates, financial values, and clinical durations aligned.
- **Tone & Contrast:** Titles adopt semi-bold and bold weights with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`), conveying poise and clarity without appearing authoritarian.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a flexible, fluid-responsive grid system paired with a rigorous 4px/8px modular spacing rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Fixed collapsable sidebar navigation (260px wide, collapses to 72px icon rail), paired with a 12-column fluid canvas (`gutter-desktop: 1.75rem`, `margin-desktop: 2.5rem`). Content max-width is constrained to `1600px` for optimal viewing on wide monitors.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column adaptive layout (`gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin-tablet: 2rem`), sidebar collapses to a slide-over drawer or compact bottom sheet for clinical consultation tablets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-flow column (`gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1rem`). Key workflows (e.g., patient check-in, agenda timeline) stack vertically.

### Density Control

The platform supports two distinct density modes:

- **Default (Comfortable):** Standard spacing for clinical therapy workflows, patient interactions, and clinical evolution notes (`space-md` to `space-xl`).
- **Compact:** Reduced padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) applied strictly to monthly multi-professional calendars, billing ledgers, and audit logs.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is deliberately soft and calming, rejecting intense, harsh drop shadows in favor of diffused ambient light and tonal separation.

### Surface Elevation Levels

- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAF9`. Pure matte background; absorbs light and reduces eye fatigue.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Records):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAF9`, outlined with a 1px continuous border of `#E2E8F0` and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.04), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Dropdowns):** Popovers, active patient selector menus, and floating datepickers utilize: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Prontuary detail dialogues and emergency consultation panels. Tinted backdrop scrim using `#1E3A4C` at 30% opacity with a `4px` blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`), paired with elevation shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 58, 76, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses **Rounded (Scale 2)** with an emphasis on soft curvature (`rounded-xl` for cards and modals) to reflect hospitality and organic safety.

### Geometric Application

- **Main Container Cards & Modals:** `1.5rem` (24px, `rounded-xl`) to soften structural containers.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `0.5rem` (8px, standard base roundedness) for structured clarity.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** `0.5rem` (8px) for primary actions; `9999px` (fully rounded pill) for patient badges, session status chips, and teleconsultation tags.
- **Avatar & Practitioner Portraits:** Circular (`rounded-full`) or squircle (`rounded-xl` with 12px smoothing) for a warm human presence.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Deep petrol background (`#1E3A4C`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border-radius, medium weight. On hover, shifts softly to `#162C3A` with a micro-elevation lift.
- **Secondary:** Sage green background (`#4E7D6B`), white text (`#FFFFFF`). On hover, transitions to `#3E6556`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, petrol text (`#1E3A4C`), subtle sage mint hover tint (`#E2EFE9`).
- **Teleconsultation Action:** Clinical Blue (`#3B82F6`) paired with an integrated video camera icon for instant affordance.

### Input Fields & Anamnesis Text Areas

- Built with a crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `#1E293B` text, and a `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in Sage Herbal (`#4E7D6B` at 30% alpha) and border `#4E7D6B`. No high-strung blue rings; maintains visual peace.
- **Clinical Evolution Note Area:** Expanded line height, integrated character and autosave indicators ("Salvo automaticamente às 14:32"), with an expandable distraction-free mode.

### Status Chips & Badges

- Compact pill-shaped structures (`padding: 4px 10px`, `font-size: 12px`, `font-weight: 600`).
- **Confirmed / Paid:** Background `#E6F7F0`, Text `#065F46`, Dot indicator `#10B981`.
- **Pending / Scheduled:** Background `#FEF3C7`, Text `#92400E`, Dot indicator `#F59E0B`.
- **Teleconsultation:** Background `#EFF6FF`, Text `#1E40AF`, Dot indicator `#3B82F6`.
- **Cancelled / Absent:** Background `#FEE2E2`, Text `#991B1B`, Dot indicator `#EF4444`.

### Cards & Patient Records

- Background: `#FFFFFF` on `#F8FAF9` base.
- Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Radius: `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`).
- Header includes clear patient demographic markers, active session counts, and quick-action menu (Prontuário, Evolução, Agendar).

### Calendar & Scheduling Matrix

- Clean white surface with muted cell dividers.
- Current hour line indicated by an unobtrusive Sage-tinted horizontal marker.
- Appointment blocks styled using soft pill containers with primary border accents indicating consultation modality (Presencial vs. Online).

### Checkboxes & Radios

- Soft square (`4px` radius) for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Inactive state: `#CBD5E1` border with pure white fill.
- Checked state: `#1E3A4C` fill with white checkmark glyph.

### Navigation Sidebar

- Deep subtle off-white or deep slate tone matching the clinic identity.
- Minimalist line iconography (Calendar, Patients, Financial, Reports, Settings) with 20px bounding boxes.
- Active item indicator: highlighted with Soft Mint background (`#E2EFE9`) and Deep Petrol typography (`#1E3A4C`) with a vertical accent bar on the left boundary.
